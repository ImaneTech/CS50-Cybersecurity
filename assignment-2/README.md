## Summary

This assignment focuses on securing networks and the systems connected to them. It covers how HTTPS and TLS certificates protect data in transit, how attackers exploit unsecured connections through packet sniffing and machine-in-the-middle attacks, and how defenses such as firewalls, VPNs, and SSH restrict unauthorized access. It also introduces penetration testing and port scanning as tools used to identify vulnerabilities before attackers do, as well as the threat posed by malware, botnets, and DDoS attacks.

## Key Takeaways

- TLS certificates, issued and verified by a Certificate Authority, establish trust and encrypt traffic between client and server, mitigating machine-in-the-middle attacks.
- HSTS prevents SSL stripping by forcing browsers to only connect over HTTPS.
- Firewalls and port scanning work as complementary tools: one restricts access, the other identifies what is exposed.
- Ethical hacking and penetration testing are essential for proactively discovering vulnerabilities before they can be exploited.
- Botnets and DDoS attacks demonstrate how compromised systems can be weaponized at scale against a single target.
